# 📊 02: RWE Causal Inference & Advanced Analytics Pipeline

A professional-grade R pipeline designed for HTA-ready analysis of Real-World Evidence (RWE), utilizing modern causal inference frameworks to mitigate confounding.

## 🚀 Pipeline Overview
- **Objective**: Estimation of Average Treatment Effect (ATE) in observational cohorts.
- **Methods**: Inverse Probability of Treatment Weighting (IPTW), Augmented IPW (AIPW), and Targeted Maximum Likelihood Estimation (TMLE).
- **Technology**: `R`, `SuperLearner`, `MatchIt`, `WeightIt`.

## 📈 Key Deliverables
- **Covariate Balancing**: Automated generation of Love Plots and standardized mean difference (SMD) reports.
- **Sensitivity Analysis**: E-value calculations to quantify the potential impact of unobserved confounding.
- **Reporting**: Audit-ready HTML reports detailing model specifications and convergence diagnostics.

*This pipeline adheres to NICE RWE framework standards.*
