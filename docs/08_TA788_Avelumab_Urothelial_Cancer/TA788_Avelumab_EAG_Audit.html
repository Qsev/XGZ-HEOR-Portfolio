TA788 EAG Audit Case: Avelumab Maintenance in Urothelial Cancer

Partitioned Survival Modelling, Treatment Effect Capping, and NICE Decision Uncertainty

Author

Xiaoge Zhang, PhD

Published

June 5, 2026

ImportantPublic Evidence and Modelling Disclosure

This portfolio case study is based on public NICE TA788 appraisal documents. The future interactive model will use transparent public assumptions and reconstructed modelling logic for educational and methodological demonstration. It will not reproduce the confidential manufacturer model, patient-level trial data, or confidential commercial discount for avelumab.

1 Project Aim

This page will become an interactive EAG-style audit of NICE TA788: avelumab for maintenance treatment of locally advanced or metastatic urothelial cancer after platinum-based chemotherapy.

The intended architecture is a Quarto page with an embedded Shinylive for R application. The static narrative will explain the health technology assessment problem, while the browser-run R/Shiny module will allow users to test how survival extrapolation, treatment effect capping, utilities, and public cost assumptions affect model outputs.

2 Decision Problem

Element Working abstraction
Population Adults with locally advanced or metastatic urothelial cancer whose disease has not progressed after platinum-based chemotherapy
Intervention Avelumab maintenance treatment
Comparator Watchful waiting / best supportive care
Key trial JAVELIN Bladder 100
Economic model Three-state partitioned survival model
Health states Progression-free, progressed disease, death
Core audit question Are the long-term survival assumptions and treatment effect cap clinically plausible and economically transparent?

3 Planned Interactive Modules

The final page will develop the model in phases:

  1. Survival extrapolation sandbox for OS and PFS.
  2. Partitioned survival state occupancy engine.
  3. Treatment effect capping after the avelumab stopping rule.
  4. Utility and cost accumulation.
  5. EAG-style warning checks for structural issues such as PFS exceeding OS.
  6. Scenario comparison between public company-facing assumptions and EAG-preferred assumptions.

5 Evidence Base

The Phase 1 fact-finding step has extracted the initial EAG audit parameters from the public NICE TA788 evidence set, including:

  • OS extrapolation dispute: company generalised gamma versus ERG log-normal.
  • PFS extrapolation dispute: BICR-assessed PFS versus investigator-assessed PFS and alternative spline models.
  • Treatment effect cap: a 5-year stopping rule with treatment effect capped 1 year after stopping.
  • Base-case utilities: progression-free and progressed disease utility values from JAVELIN Bladder 100 EQ-5D data.
  • Public list price and administration cost assumptions for avelumab.

The next development step is to build the standalone R mathematical engine before embedding it in Shinylive.
